Toll Charges & Trip Multipliers: Standard vehicles pay their base tariff multiplied by trip type (Single: 1x, Double: 2x, Triple: 3x).

Waiting-Time Toll Waiver Rule: Under consumer protection and service quality rules, if a vehicle's waiting time in queue exceeds its category limit (e.g., 5 min for Two-wheelers, 10 min for Four-wheelers), the toll fee is completely waived (₹0).

Violations & Penalties: Horn violations (> 5 honks) incur a ₹200 fine. Pollution violations incur a ₹500 fine.

Emergency Vehicles: Ambulances (🚑) and Fire Brigades (🚒) enjoy full toll exemption (₹0), zero service delay (0 min visit), and all violation fines are strictly waived.

Local & Government Exemptions: Government vehicles (🚘), Government buses (🚌), and Exempted locals (🎫) receive category-based toll exemption (₹0). Note that local exemption is categorized by vehicle classification rather than live geolocation verification.

Project Origin: Developed originally for B.Sc. Computer Science Semester V practicals, now modernized with an intelligent multi-lane highway operations dashboard.

Simulation Architecture: Uses an asynchronous event timeline and continuous kinematic positioning. Vehicles approach along designated lane centerlines, hold steady queue intervals, advance through the toll booth barrier, and depart down the exit corridor.

Deployment: 100% client-side web application with zero external build dependencies. Compatible with Netlify, Vercel, and GitHub Pages.
